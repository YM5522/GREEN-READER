How To Use

GREEN READER — GOLF GREEN SLOPE ANALYZER

GREEN READER uses your iPhone's motion sensor to measure golf green slope and estimate putt break. Perfect for practicing green reading on the practice green.

Basic Usage

1

Enable Sensor

Tap "Enable Sensor" on the splash screen and allow access to the motion sensor.

2

Place iPhone on the Green

Place your iPhone flat on the green facing toward the cup. Once the bubble stabilizes, slope measurement is complete.

3

Set Distance and Stimp

Set the distance from ball to cup (paced off) and the stimp value for the green. Tap the field to open the drum-roll picker.

4

Read the Estimated Break

Follow the "EST. BREAK" value and the "X cups aim right/left" instruction to line up your putt.

Reading the Display

LEFT / RIGHT
Lateral Slope

Right high → positive
Left high → negative

FRONT / BACK
Vertical Slope

Uphill → positive
Downhill → negative

TOTAL
Combined Slope

Combined angle of lateral and vertical slope

BREAK LINE
Break Type

6 types: uphill/downhill × hook/slice/straight

Break Types

UPHILL STRAIGHT UPHILL HOOK UPHILL SLICE DOWNHILL STRAIGHT DOWNHILL HOOK DOWNHILL SLICE

Hook: Right side high, left side low → ball breaks left → aim right

Slice: Left side high, right side low → ball breaks right → aim left

Just Touch / Firm +30cm

Just Touch
Dying at the Cup

Ball just reaches the cup. Maximum break effect from slope.

Firm +30cm
30cm Past the Cup

Slightly firm pace. Less break compared to just touch.

Wind Feature (WIND)

Turn WIND ON to automatically fetch local wind direction and speed, and factor it into the break calculation.

TIP: Point your iPhone toward the cup while using the app so the compass correctly calculates headwind, tailwind, and crosswind. The compass arrow shows the direction the wind is coming from.

What is Stimp?

Stimp (Stimpmeter) measures green speed in feet. Higher values mean faster greens where the ball rolls farther.
