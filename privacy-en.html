Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

GREEN READER (the "App") is a web application for measuring golf green slope. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the App.

Information We Access

The App accesses the following information:

  • Motion Sensor Data: Used to measure device tilt for slope calculation. This data is processed locally on your device and is never sent to any server.
  • Location (Optional): Only when the Wind feature is turned ON, your current location is used to fetch local weather data. Location is sent to the Open-Meteo weather API but is not stored by this App.

How We Use Information

  • Measuring green slope and calculating estimated break
  • Wind speed/direction correction for break calculation (Wind feature only)

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): When Wind is ON, your location is sent to retrieve weather data.
  • Google AdSense: May use cookies for ad personalization. See Google Privacy Policy for details.
  • Google Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google servers.

Cookies

The App itself does not use cookies. However, Google AdSense may use cookies for ad delivery. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

Data Storage

The App does not store any personal information on a server. All measurement data is processed locally on your device.

Analytics

The App may use analytics tools to improve the service. Data collected is anonymized and cannot identify individuals.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated without prior notice. Changes take effect upon posting to this page.

Contact

For inquiries regarding this policy, please contact us via the App's GitHub page.
